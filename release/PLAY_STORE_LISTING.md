# LEARNERS DIC. — Play Store listing draft

**Short description**

A thoughtful dictionary and structured language practice in six languages.

**Full description**

LEARNERS DIC. is a quiet place to look up words, practise a language, and build a steady study habit.

Explore English, French, Spanish, Yorùbá, Igbo, and Hausa. Search available dictionary entries, hear words with voices on your device, save entries for offline review, and study a seven-lesson introduction for each language. Lessons include vocabulary, model phrases, writing prompts, model answers, and recall practice.

Dictionary coverage varies by word and language. Search results come from community-maintained Wiktionary entries when available, and the app reports when it cannot find a sourced entry. Only entries you open are cached for offline review. The lesson materials are introductory and should be checked with qualified speakers, especially for regional usage and pronunciation.

The optional translator sends the text you enter to MyMemory. Its terms say submitted text may be kept long term and processed with service partners. Do not use it for personal, private, or confidential text. Machine translations may be inaccurate.

LEARNERS DIC. does not require an account and stores saved words, lesson progress, and recent translations on your device.
