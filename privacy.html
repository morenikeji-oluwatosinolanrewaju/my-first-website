LEARNERS DIC. · A WORLD OF WORDS

Privacy Policy

Updated 17 November 2026

In brief: your saved words and learning progress stay on your device. Dictionary lookups and translation text are sent to the named third-party services when you use those features. LEARNERS DIC. has no account system, cloud sync, ads, or app analytics.

Information stored on your device

Saved words, opened dictionary entries, lesson progress, recent translations, and your translation disclosure choice are stored locally on your device. LEARNERS DIC. does not operate an account or synchronization service. You can clear recent translations from the Translator screen. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes other local app data. Device backup behavior is controlled by your operating system.

Dictionary searches

When a word is not already saved on your device, the word and selected language are sent over HTTPS to English Wiktionary at en.wiktionary.org. Wikimedia may process ordinary connection information such as your IP address under its own privacy practices. Opened entries are cached locally for offline review. See the Wikimedia privacy policy.

Translations

If you choose to translate, the text and language pair are sent over HTTPS to MyMemory, operated by Translated S.r.l. MyMemory’s current terms say submitted segments may be stored long term and may be processed with service partners. LEARNERS DIC. does not control or delete information held by MyMemory. Do not enter passwords, personal messages, health or financial details, or other private or confidential text. Read MyMemory’s terms before using this feature.

Speech playback, ads, and analytics

Speech playback uses the text-to-speech voices supplied by your device. The operating system or voice provider may handle speech requests under its own terms. LEARNERS DIC. includes no advertising or app analytics SDKs.

Retention and choices

Your recent translation history can be cleared in the app. Saved words, learning progress, and cached entries can be removed by clearing LEARNERS DIC. app storage or uninstalling the app. Requests about translations already sent to MyMemory must be directed to MyMemory; LEARNERS DIC. cannot remove a provider’s copy.

Contact

For privacy questions or correction requests, contact the LEARNERS DIC. project maintainers through the public repository issue tracker. Do not include private messages, passwords, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

LEARNERS DIC. · This policy applies to the LEARNERS DIC. mobile app.
